New to importing? How this works +
A vehicle can be imported to Australia when it appears on the SEVS register (Specialist and Enthusiast Vehicle Scheme) or the MRE list (Model Report Eligibility), within its approved build range, and a registered workshop can comply it. Use the filters above to search by model, chassis code, criterion, or the year you want it built in. Eligible means it currently qualifies. Expired entries are kept for reference.
