Understanding import eligibility

The plain-English version of SEVS, MRE and what makes a car importable.

What is the SEVS register?+

The Specialist & Enthusiast Vehicle Scheme (SEVS) is an Australian Government register of vehicles eligible for import. Vehicles must fit into certain criteria such as Performance, Environmental, Rarity, Campervan/Motorhome or Mobility. Vehicles under the Performance or Environmental criteria must not have been sold new in Australia, as well as other requirements. A vehicle must have an entry on the SEVS register as well as a corresponding Model Report (MRE) to be imported and complied in Australia.

What is a Model Report?+

A Model Report (MRE) is a document that describes how a vehicle on the SEV register may be modified to comply with Australian Design Rules (ADR). The work described in a Model Report is followed by a Registered Automotive Workshop (RAW) and then checked by an Authorised Vehicle Verifier (AVV) before being signed off and completed.

How do I check if my car is eligible to import to Australia?+

Search this register by make, model, chassis code, or the year you want the car built in. A green ELIGIBLE badge means the model is currently approved. Always confirm with an import agent before you buy. Or ask JDM Connect for a free eligibility check.

How current is this eligibility data?+

It is pulled directly from the Australian Government ROVER portal (the SEVS Register and Model Report Approvals) and refreshed daily. This register is a guide only, always confirm eligibility with an import agent before purchase.
