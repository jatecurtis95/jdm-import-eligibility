New to importing? How this works +
1. It’s on the list. Every car here has been approved by the Australian Government as eligible to import — on the SEVS register or as a Model Report (MRE). 2. We land it. JDM Connect sources the car in Japan and ships it to Australia. 3. It’s made road-ready. A licensed RAWS compliance workshop certifies it, then it’s registered like any other car. Use the filters to search by model, chassis code, criterion, or the year you want it built in.
