Understanding import eligibility

The plain-English version of SEVS, MRE and what makes a car importable.

What is the SEVS register?

The Specialist & Enthusiast Vehicle Scheme (SEVS) is an Australian Government register of vehicle models approved for import because they are rare, high-performance, or otherwise not sold new in Australia. If a model is on the SEVS register it can be imported and complied for road registration.

What is the difference between SEVS and a Model Report (MRE)?

A SEVS entry says a model is eligible to import. A Model Report (MRE) is a RAWS workshop's approved compliance package for a specific model, the practical recipe to make it road-legal in Australia. A car with both a SEVS listing and a Model Report is the most straightforward to import.

How do I check if my car is eligible to import to Australia?

Search this register by make, model, chassis code, or the year you want the car built in. A green ELIGIBLE badge means the model is currently approved. Always confirm with a licensed RAWS workshop before you buy. Or ask JDM Connect for a free eligibility check.

How current is this eligibility data?

It is pulled directly from the Australian Government ROVER portal (the SEVS Register and Model Report Approvals) and refreshed daily. This register is a guide only, always confirm eligibility with a licensed RAWS workshop before purchase.
